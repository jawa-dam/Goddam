🧠 Tracking Integrity

V6.12 records information supplied by the researcher about a submission or its status. “Submitted,” “under review,” “accepted,” and other statuses are user-recorded states; the engine does not verify them. No journal, publisher, reviewer, or external service is contacted automatically.
