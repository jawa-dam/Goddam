Preserve the Documentation Behind a Submission Record

V6.13 creates a local evidence record for submission-related documents, correspondence, confirmations, decisions, revision requests, or other researcher-supplied artifacts. It stores descriptive metadata only; it does not upload or verify files or external events.

2️⃣ Evidence Record

3️⃣ Evidence Vault

🧠 Evidence Integrity

V6.13 records researcher-supplied descriptions of submission evidence. A saved record does not prove that an event occurred, authenticate a document, verify an external status, or contact a journal, publisher, reviewer, or other service. No file is uploaded automatically.
