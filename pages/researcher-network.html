NETWORK FOUNDATION

Connect Researchers & Knowledge

This v3.0 foundation prepares GEI Academy for multiple researcher profiles, shared research, discovery, and collaboration.

🧭 Network Principles

👤 Researcher Identity
Each researcher maintains an authored identity and portfolio.
🧠 Knowledge
Knowledge records become discoverable research objects.
🔬 Investigations
Case Files connect evidence, experiments, models, and conclusions.
📚 Publications
Published research becomes part of the discovery layer.
🤝 Collaboration
Future network layers can connect researchers around shared questions and investigations.

🔐 Privacy Foundation

The current Academy remains local-first. v3.0 does not expose private research data to other users or claim that local records are remotely published. Future server-backed networking should require explicit publishing and access controls.
